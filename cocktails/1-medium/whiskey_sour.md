# Whiskey Sour

Courtesy of every speakeasy that ever talked a big game about "balance"

## Ingredients

- 2 Measures Bourbon or Rye Whiskey
- 3/4 Measure Fresh Lemon Juice
- 3/4 Measure Simple Syrup
- 1 Egg White (Optional)
- 3-4 Ice Cubes
- A Maraschino Cherry & Orange Slice (Optional)

## Method

1. Add the whiskey, lemon juice, syrup, and egg white to a shaker WITHOUT ice
2. Shake hard for 15 seconds to build the foam
3. Add ice to the shaker and shake again until ice-cold
4. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice
5. Drop in the cherry and orange slice for a bit of pizzaz (pizzaz optional)
6. Enjoy!
